# 🎬 Sonarr & Radarr PVR Ecosystem (stevietv)
> **Automated Smart TV & Movie PVR Stack**
- **Repos:** `stevietv/Sonarr`, `stevietv/Radarr`
- **Tech Stack:** C#, .NET, SQLite, Torrent/Usenet APIs
- **Key Features:**
  - Tracks incoming TV series and movie releases.
  - Connects to download clients (qBittorrent, Transmission, SABnzbd).
  - Organizes and renames media files automatically.
